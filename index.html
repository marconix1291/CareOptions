About Deni Berry, FNP

I licensed in 2017 and continue to love what I do! I raised two wonderful sons who are fondly remembered. I am very happily married, live in the country and am slowly learning to not be afraid of horses and cows! We have four grandchildren and three dogs so far. I enjoy cooking, gardening and reading in addition to spending time with my husband, family and friends.

My Philosophy

Health is so much more than not being sick. Health is a state of physical, mental, and spiritual/social well-being. I strive to implement this holistic approach to the care of every patient. My practice is a blend of Western and integrative/functional measures. There is no "one-size fits all." I challenge myself to think outside of the box, learning always, and will work hard for you while encouraging you to be an active participant in your care.
